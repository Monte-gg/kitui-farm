About us

Bringing Kitui farmers and local buyers closer.

KITUI FARM is an online marketplace where farmers can share the produce they have available and customers can discover food offered by local farms.

What we do

We provide a place for farmers to create produce listings with details such as price, quantity, location, photos, and videos. Buyers can browse those listings and place orders through the marketplace.

Why KITUI FARM

We want it to be easier for people to find local produce and for farmers to show what is ready on their farms. Clear listing details help buyers and farmers understand what is available.

For farmers

Create an account, add your produce, and keep your listings up to date so customers can see what you have to offer.

For buyers

Explore available produce, compare listing details, and choose items that suit your needs.
