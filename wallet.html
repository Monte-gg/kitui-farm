How deposits and withdrawals work

  1. Deposit: enter the amount and your M-Pesa number, then approve the STK Push prompt with your PIN. The wallet is credited only after Safaricom confirms the payment.
  2. Withdraw: the amount is reserved from your balance the moment you request it, so it cannot be spent twice. Daraja then pays it to your M-Pesa number.
  3. Status: every request shows its Safaricom reference and result in the list below. Refresh or keep this page open; it updates by itself.
