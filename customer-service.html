Common questions

How do I list produce as a farmer?

Sign in to your farmer account, open the farmer dashboard, and use the listing form to add produce details and a photo or video.

How do I place an order?

Sign in as a buyer, browse produce on the marketplace, add what you need to your cart, and continue to checkout.

Who can help with an order or payment?

Contact customer service using the call or WhatsApp buttons above and include the order details you need help with.

How do I update my account details?

Open your profile from the dashboard to change your name, phone number, location, profile photo, or gender.

How do I add money to my wallet?

Contact Customer Service for payment instructions, then submit your M-PESA receipt on the Wallet page. Funds are added after an administrator verifies the payment.
