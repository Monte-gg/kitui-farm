Install KITUI FARM

Get the app on your phone — works like a download, no store needed.

  1. Open KITUI FARM in your phone's browser (Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone).
  2. Tap the Share or menu button.
  3. Choose "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Open it from your home screen — it runs full-screen like an app.
